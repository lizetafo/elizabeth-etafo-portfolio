    Ph.D. Candidate and Graduate Research Assistant

    CNS efficacy of antiretroviral therapy

    Conducted preclinical studies in a humanized microglial mouse model. Combined ddPCR, multicolor flow cytometry, IHC, IF, multispectral microscopy, quantitative image analysis, and LC-MS/MS pharmacokinetic data to connect molecular, cellular, pathologic, and drug-exposure outcomes. Mentored undergraduate students.

    Polymeric nanoparticle drug delivery

    Formulated and optimized polymeric nanoparticle systems using solvent diffusion and self-assembly. Characterized size, dispersity, surface charge, and composition with DLS, zeta potential, and NMR, with dialysis-based purification and encapsulation-efficiency assessment.

    Viral infectivity profiling of HIV strains

    Generated infectious HIV strain stocks through bacterial transformation, plasmid DNA purification, and mammalian-cell transfection. Used TZM-bl luciferase reporter assays to quantify strain-specific infectivity and evaluate assay performance.

    Interactive RNA-seq analysis application

    Built an R Shiny application that takes gene-count matrices and sample metadata and runs DESeq2 normalization, differential expression, and gene annotation, with input validation, configurable comparisons, downloadable results, and volcano plots, heatmaps, and ranked-gene plots.
