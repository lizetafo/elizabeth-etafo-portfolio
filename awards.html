Awards and scholarships

2026 · CROI 2027

New Researcher Scholarship

Conference on Retroviruses and Opportunistic Infections (CROI)

CROI is the world’s premier scientific meeting on HIV and related infectious diseases, where leading researchers and clinicians present breakthrough findings. The New Researcher Scholarship is a competitive award recognizing early-career investigators for the strength of their research, granted to presenting authors of accepted abstracts. It includes participation in the Scott M. Hammer Workshop for New Researchers and Trainees.

2026

Best Abstract Award

AAPS PharmSci 360, American Association of Pharmaceutical Scientists

AAPS PharmSci 360 is the annual meeting of the American Association of Pharmaceutical Scientists. The Best Abstract Award recognizes the top 10% of submissions from graduate students and early-career scientists, judged on novelty, data-driven conclusions, and advancement of the field. Awarded for my work showing regimen-specific differences in HIV control in the brain.

2026

Nancy and Ronald Reagan Alzheimer’s Scholarship

Kinman-Oldfield Family Foundation, UNMC

An annual award named for President Ronald Reagan, who lived with Alzheimer’s disease, recognizing promising and innovative Alzheimer’s research by UNMC investigators. Awarded for my research on links between HIV infection and Alzheimer’s-type brain changes.

2026

Outstanding Oral Presentation Award

Neuroscience Symposium

Awarded to the best oral presentations at the symposium, as judged on scientific merit, clarity and organization of the talk, and the ability to answer questions from the audience and judges.

2026

Dean of Graduate Studies Conference Attendance Award

University of Nebraska Medical Center

A UNMC Graduate Studies award that supports graduate students in presenting their research at scientific conferences, matched by departmental funding.
