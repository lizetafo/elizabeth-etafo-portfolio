Peer-reviewed publications

Brain, Behavior, and Immunity2026 · Review

Neuroimmune Crosstalk in HIV-Associated Neurocognitive Disorders and the Impact of Antiretroviral Therapy

E. Etafo, et al.

Explains why HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders persist even when antiretroviral therapy controls the virus in the blood. The review focuses on microglia as long-lived HIV reservoirs in the brain, the chronic glial inflammation that injures neurons and synapses, and how antiretroviral drugs themselves can influence brain vulnerability.

Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews2026 · Review

Does HIV-1 Infection Drive Alzheimer’s Disease Pathobiology?

E. Etafo, et al.

As people with HIV live longer on antiretroviral therapy, the risk of age-related brain disease rises. This review examines the overlap between HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders and Alzheimer’s disease: shared neuroinflammation, microglial activation and amyloid-β changes, how antiretroviral therapy may affect Alzheimer’s-like pathology, and the new models used to study the link.

NeuroImmune Pharmacology and Therapeutics2025

Persistent HIV and Associated Pathology in the Spinal Cord of ART-Treated HIV-Infected Humanized Glial Mice

E. Etafo, A. Fernandes, E. Makarov, M. Thiele, D. P. Gnanadhas, D. Datta, S. Gorantla

Abstracts of the 29th Scientific Conference of the Society on NeuroImmune Pharmacology (SNIP), Omaha, NE. Used a humanized glial mouse model to show that HIV persists in the spinal cord despite antiretroviral therapy, a part of the central nervous system that is studied far less than the brain, with associated neuroinflammation and pathology.
